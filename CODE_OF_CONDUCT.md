# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want every project here to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience,
background, identity or the language they write in. Turkish and English are both welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not people.
- Keep discussions focused on the problem being solved.
- Help newcomers find their way around the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the affected repository's **Security** tab
(**Report a vulnerability**). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, code or issues that break this code
of conduct, and may block contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
